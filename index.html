About Us

About Us

Sam's Tune Up Masters is a family-owned repair shop. We service vehicles of all makes and models and provide quality repair and maintenance services at affordable prices. The satisfaction of our customers is our highest priority.

  • Oil Change

    Oil change intervals can vary depending on your vehicle age, type of oil and driving conditions. With modern lubricants most engines today have recommended oil change intervals of 5,000 to 7,500 miles.

  • Transmission Service

    Service intervals for an automatic transmission vary from every 30,000 miles to 100,000 miles. Manufacturers recommend that manual transmission fluid be changed every 30,000 to 60,000 miles.

  • Smog

    We Smog Test all Cars, SUV’s, Trucks, Vans, RV’s. We provide DMV smog check, Test only smog check, Out of State smog testing, Diesel smog check, Title transfer smog
